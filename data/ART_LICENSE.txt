Assets in this directory
========================

`soldier_red_front.png`, `soldier_blue_front.png`, `soldier_green_front.png`,
`soldier_yellow_front.png`
    Ledger's four sprite kits. These are nano-banana (Gemini
    `gemini-2.5-flash-image`) renders of the Softmax cog, generated from one
    sheet so the four kits share a style, then chroma-keyed and split. Each
    cog carries one large, high-contrast prop — a ledger book, a coin sack, a
    balance scale, a parchment and quill — so two avatars are tellable apart
    at board scale with the alias plates hidden.

    Source sheet: `scripts/art/source/cogs_sheet.png`
    Generator:    `scripts/art/gen_cog_sheet.py`
    Splitter:     `scripts/art/split_cog_sheet.py` (owns these four files)

    The file names are cogame-babel's, kept because `client/renderer.js`,
    `tools/build_replay_viewer.sh` and the server's `/client/assets` route all
    refer to them.
